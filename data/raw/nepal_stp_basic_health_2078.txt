# Standard Treatment Protocol (STP) for Basic Health Services (BHS) Package, 2078 - Nepal
## Curative Service Division, Ministry of Health and Population (MoHP)

### Overview
Comprehensive clinical guidelines for managing common health conditions at the basic health care level in Nepal.

### Part One: General Information
- People-Centered Care: Focus on patient-centered approaches.
- Rational Prescribing: Principles for appropriate use of drugs and antibiotics.
- Prescription Standards: Structure of a proper medical prescription.
- Infection Control: Protocols for prevention, control, and waste management.

### Part Two: Common Emergencies and Symptoms
- Emergency Conditions:
    - Surgical skills: Dressing, suturing, incision and drainage.
    - Acute states: Shock, unconsciousness, convulsions.
    - Trauma: Primary Trauma Care (PTC), fracture/dislocation management, burns, drowning.
    - Specifics: Snake bites, poisoning.
- Common Symptoms:
    - Pain management (Chest pain, abdominal pain, headache).
    - Respiratory: Breathlessness, cough.
    - Gastrointestinal: Nausea, vomiting.
    - General: Fever, dizziness, vertigo, syncope, itching, fatigue.
- Specialty Emergencies: ENT and Eye emergencies.

### Part Three: Preventive and Promotive Health Services
- National Programs:
    - National Immunization Program (NIP).
    - Integrated Management of Neonatal and Childhood Illness (IMNCI).
- Nutrition: Malnutrition management, growth monitoring, micro-nutrient supplementation.
- Maternal and Reproductive Health:
    - Antenatal Care (ANC), anemia in pregnancy.
    - Labor and birth, obstetric first aid.
    - Safe abortion services, PMTCT.
    - Family planning, cervical cancer screening.
- Disease Management:
    - Communicable: HIV/AIDS, Malaria, Leprosy, Kala-azar, Dengue, Lymphatic Filariasis.
    - Non-Communicable Diseases (NCDs): Management and screening.
- Additional: Common skin, oral, ear, nose, and throat conditions.

Note: This 310-page document is the standard for free basic health services in Nepal.
