# National Kala-azar Elimination Protocol - Nepal
## Epidemiology and Disease Control Division (EDCD)

### Goal
Reduce the incidence of Kala-azar (Visceral Leishmaniasis) to less than 1 per 10,000 population at the district level.

### Diagnosis
- Primary Tool: **rK39 rapid diagnostic test (RDT)**.
- Features: Simple, sensitive, and field-based detection.

### Treatment Protocol
- **First-line therapy**: Single-dose infusion of **Liposomal Amphotericin B (L-AmB)**.
- Historical Note: Replaced oral Miltefosine due to teratogenicity and relapse concerns.

### Surveillance & Control
- Integrated Vector Management: Indoor residual spraying (IRS) with insecticides in endemic areas (sandfly control).
- Case Detection: Active case detection and timely reporting via KA Tracker (DHIS2).
- Pharmacovigilance: Monitoring safety and efficacy of anti-leishmanial drugs.
