# Nepal Maternal Health Guidelines
## Family Welfare Division, Ministry of Health and Population (MoHP)

### Vision
To ensure the right to safe motherhood and reproductive health as a fundamental right, as enshrined in the Constitution of Nepal.

### Strategic Goals (Road Map 2030)
- Reduce Maternal Mortality Ratio (MMR) to 70 per 100,000 live births by 2030 (SDG target).
- Reduce neonatal mortality.

### Legal Framework
- Right to Safe Motherhood and Reproductive Health Act (2018).

### Core Services
- **Continuum of Care**: 
    - Antenatal Care (ANC).
    - Skilled attendance during labor and childbirth.
    - Postnatal Care (PNC).
- **Accessibility**: Services are part of the Basic Health Service (BHS) package and are provided **free of charge** at public health facilities.

### Priority Areas
- Quality of Care: Ensuring "respectful maternal care."
- Equity: Addressing geographical, economic, and social disparities.
- Emergency Care: Strengthening referral mechanisms and Emergency Obstetric Care (EmOC) for high-risk pregnancies.
